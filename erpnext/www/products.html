Key Features

Everything you need to manage your printing jobs efficiently

📊

Real-Time Job Tracking

Track every job from order receipt to delivery with visual status indicators. Know exactly what's happening with each job at any moment.

🔢

Custom Job Numbering

Automatic job numbers with customer abbreviations (e.g., GRP-0001-FEB-2026). Easy to identify and track jobs for each customer.

👥

Customer Portal

Give customers 24/7 access to track their jobs online. Reduce calls and improve transparency with self-service tracking.

💬

WhatsApp Notifications

Automatic WhatsApp alerts to customers when job status changes. Send from your own WhatsApp Business number.

🏢

Multi-Tenant Support

Perfect for managing multiple printing companies or branches under one system. Each tenant has separate data and branding.

📱

Mobile Responsive

Access from any device - desktop, tablet, or smartphone. Manage jobs on the go with our mobile-friendly interface.

📈

Reports & Analytics

Comprehensive reports on job status, delivery performance, customer activity, and business metrics.

⚙️

Workflow Automation

Automate status updates, notifications, and approvals. Save time with smart workflow rules.

🎨

Custom Branding

Add your company logo, colors, and branding to customer portal. White-label solution for professional appearance.
